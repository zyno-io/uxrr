## How to Sign

**Individuals.** Open a pull request on [github.com/zyno-io/uxrr](https://github.com/zyno-io/uxrr). The CLA bot will ask You to post the following comment on the pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Posting that comment from Your GitHub account is Your electronic signature of Part A. It is recorded with Your GitHub username, the pull request, and the date, and it covers all of Your present and future Contributions. You only need to sign once per version of this Agreement.

**Entities.** An authorized representative completes the signature block at the end of Part B and emails it to support@sgnl24.com from an address at the entity’s domain, including the list of GitHub usernames of its Designated Contributors (Schedule A). The Licensor will confirm receipt. Each Designated Contributor still signs Part A by posting the comment above on their first pull request.
